# Routine Content Sources

This project's routine wording and scoring guidance are aligned to widely used snooker coaching terminology and official rules references.

Primary references:

- WPBSA Rules of Snooker and English Billiards (official rulebook link):
  - https://www.wpbsa.com/rules/
- WPBSA coaching pathway and accredited coaching framework:
  - https://www.wpbsa.com/participation/coaching/
- WST as the official professional competition body context:
  - https://www.wst.tv/

Routine-specific terminology and drill naming are aligned to established coaching usage from the tutorial sources already attached in-app for each routine (for example: Shaun Murphy Snooker, Stephen Hendry's Cue Tips, Barry Stark Snooker Coach, BartonSnooker).

Notes on scoring conventions used in app content:

- Foundation drills prioritize repeatable technique checkpoints and session consistency.
- Break-building drills use visit quality and conversion metrics where no true fixed ceiling exists.
- Session caps are only shown where a routine has a defined finite attempt structure.

Progress tracking approach:

- The app intentionally avoids fixed target scores for routines.
- Players are encouraged to log results and define their own benchmarks based on level, coaching input, and current training goals.
