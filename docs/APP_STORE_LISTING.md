## Description (4,000 max)

> Snookered is the all-in-one app for snooker players. Score your matches, practise with purpose, see how you are really getting on, play the people at your club, and get coaching - on your game or on running your own coaching business.
>
> SCORE EVERY FRAME
> Tap the balls as they go down. Snookered keeps the score, the breaks, the fouls and the points left on the table, knows when snookers are needed, and settles the frame when it is done. Prefer to just enter the result? That works too. Every match gets an overview with the numbers that matter, and a result card to share that leads with the big moment: the century, the maximum, the comeback.
>
> PRACTISE WITH A PLAN
> A library of proper routines, from the line-up to the colours, each with its own progress chart and personal best. Build your own routines on a table diagram, plan your week, set goals and keep a streak going.
>
> SEE YOUR PROGRESS
> Your streak, your week and your form at a glance. High breaks, centuries, head-to-heads and your practice rhythm, without wading through charts. Earn levels and achievements for the things that matter.
>
> PLAY YOUR FRIENDS
> Add the people you play. Matches against friends count for both of you once they confirm the score, so head-to-heads are ones you both agree on. Follow each other's matches live, frame by frame, as they are scored.
>
> YOUR CLUB IN YOUR POCKET
> Start a group for your league or your Tuesday night crowd, with its own chat, a feed of wins and centuries, pinned routines and leaderboards. Share routines with a link or a QR code and see who has the best score on every one.
>
> SNOOKERED COACH
> Film yourself at the table and the coach watches it, then tells you what to work on and which routines will help.
>
> FIND A COACH
> Book a verified coach near you for one-to-one lessons, message them beforehand, and keep every booking in one place.
>
> COACH DIARY
> Approved to coach? Run your diary in the app: a booking calendar, a client list (including walk-ins with no account of their own), broadcast groups for drills, videos and PDFs, and private session notes.
>
> SCAN A SNOOKER
> Missed and need to put the balls back? Scan the table with your phone's camera and Snookered places AR markers exactly where each ball was, so you can replace them for the re-rack. Works best on iPhones with a LiDAR scanner; a table diagram is there as a fallback on other phones.
>
> PRO TOUR NEWS
> The latest from the World Snooker Tour and BBC Sport, next to your own game.
>
> TOURNAMENTS
> Run a knockout or a league for your club, with the draw, fixtures and results kept for you.
>
> YOU ARE IN CONTROL
> Choose who can find you, who sees your stats and who can message you. Block or report anyone. Turn off sharing your results or live matches at any time.
>
> PLANS
> Snookered is free to start. Half-Century and Century plans raise your monthly match, tournament and coaching review limits and unlock the full routine library and analytics, billed monthly or annually. A separate Coach plan unlocks the booking diary for approved coaches. Payment is charged to your Apple account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel them in your Apple account settings.
>

> Privacy Policy: https://snookeredapp.com/privacy

## Notes for App Review

> Snookered needs an account. Two demo logins cover everything below:
>
> Player account - Email: ____________  Password: ____________ (matches, practice and a group already set up)
> Coach account - Email: ____________  Password: ____________ (already approved to coach, with a diary, a client and a booking already set up)
>
> Coach status is reached from Profile > Apply to coach, then reviewed by an admin against a word-filtered application form - the coach demo account above is pre-approved so you don't need to wait on that review to test Coach Diary and Find a Coach.
>
> Scan a Snooker (the AR table scan under a match's foul options) needs an iPhone with a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro/Pro Max, or an iPad Pro). On a device or the simulator without LiDAR it falls back to a table diagram automatically - there is nothing to toggle.
>
> Community features include a word filter on profiles, messages, groups and shared routines. Players can report or block anyone from their profile, a message (long-press), a group or a routine. Content reported by three different players is hidden automatically until it is reviewed, and reports go to an in-app admin queue that we check daily. Users can control who can find and message them in Community > Settings. Account deletion is in Profile > Settings > Data management.
>
> Live scores: a player scoring a match can share it live with friends and group members. It can be turned off from the scoring screen.

Fill in both demo logins yourself. The test accounts already have the seeded data described above.
