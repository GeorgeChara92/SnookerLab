Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. Data We Process

  • Account details such as email, username, and profile metadata.
  • Practice and match records you create in the app.
  • AI Coach clip metadata and generated feedback.
  • Subscription status and entitlement metadata.

2. How Data Is Used

  • Provide core app functionality and sync your progress.
  • Enforce plan limits and manage subscription features.
  • Improve reliability, security, and support handling.

3. Data Storage and Security

SnookerLab uses cloud infrastructure for account and feature data. Access is scoped to your account and protected by authentication and policy controls.

4. Your Controls

  • Reset password from Account Settings.
  • Resend verification email from Account Settings.
  • Delete your account directly from Account Settings.

5. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us at support@snookerlab.app.
