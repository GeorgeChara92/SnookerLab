Score

Every frame, ball by ball.

Tap the balls as they go down. Snooker Lab keeps the score, the breaks, the fouls and the points left on the table, knows when snookers are needed, and settles the frame when it is done.

  • Live scoring or just the result, whichever suits the night
  • Breaks, centuries and personal bests picked out automatically
  • A match overview the moment the last frame ends, ready to share
  • Scores save without signal and sync when you are back online

Practise

Practice with a point to it.

A library of proper routines, from the line-up to the colours, each with its own progress chart and personal best. Plan your week, set goals, keep a streak going, and build your own routines on a table diagram.

  • Progress and personal bests for every routine
  • Weekly plans, goals and streaks
  • Build routines of your own and share them with a link or QR code
  • A leaderboard on every routine: see who has the best line-up

Play

Your club, in your pocket.

Play a friend and the match counts for both of you once they confirm the score. Follow their matches live, frame by frame. Start a group for your league or your Tuesday night crowd, with its own chat, feed and leaderboards.

  • Head-to-heads that both players agree on
  • Follow friends’ matches live as they are scored
  • Groups with chat, a feed of wins and centuries, and pinned routines
  • You choose who sees your stats and who can message you
